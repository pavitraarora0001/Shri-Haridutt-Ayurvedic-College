# AYURVEDIC HERITAGE & BOTANICAL IMAGE AUDIT
## Shri Haridutt Ayurvedic Medical College & Hospital
### Visual Direction: Ancient Ayurvedic Wisdom × Natural Healing × Indian Heritage

---

## 1. AUDIT PURPOSE & SCOPE

This audit comprehensively evaluates all image assets and visual references across the entire Shri Haridutt Ayurvedic Medical College & Hospital web ecosystem.

The objective was to identify and eliminate:
- Generic college campus photography.
- Sterile western hospital interiors and clinical corridors.
- Stock doctor-and-patient portraits.
- Repetitive generic open book assets.
- Misaligned or commercial stock aesthetics.

The visual direction was transitioned exclusively to:
- **Photorealistic Ayurvedic Medicinal Herbs** (macro photography of roots, leaves, seeds, barks).
- **Authentic Botanical Gardens** (sunlit Charak Vatika, dew-covered foliage, medicinal canopies).
- **Ancient Ayurvedic Heritage** (authentic Sanskrit manuscripts, Charaka and Sushruta treatises, vintage botanical records).
- **Traditional Ayurvedic Medicine Preparation** (stone Khalwa Yantra mortars, dried roots in wooden bowls, herbal oil infusions).
- **Ancient Indian Architecture** (traditional carved sandstone arches, shaded Gurukul courtyards, heritage colonnades).
- **Traditional Panchakarma Therapy Suites** (bronze Shirodhara vessels, carved teakwood Droni therapy tables, medicated herbal oils).
