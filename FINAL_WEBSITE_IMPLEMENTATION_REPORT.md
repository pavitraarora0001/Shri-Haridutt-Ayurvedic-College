## 1. PROJECT COMPLETION SUMMARY

The comprehensive redesign of the information architecture, main navigation system, dropdown hierarchies, internal hubs, and institutional regulatory centers for **Shri Haridutt Ayurvedic Medical College & Hospital** has been successfully completed.

### Deliverables Completed:
1. **9-Pillar Main Navigation (`Navbar.tsx`)**:
   - Home, About Us, Documents, Academics, Research, Hospital, News & Events, Examination, Miscellaneous.
   - Designed with RAU-inspired deep navy dropdown backgrounds (`#0B2B4A`), antique gold separators (`#C5A46D`/30), and gold active/hover typography.
   - Dual-column structured menus for complex master divisions.
   - Full-width mobile navigation drawer with collapsible accordions.
2. **New Master Institutional Hubs & Pages**:
   - `/documents` — Master document vault with search, category filtering, and direct PDF downloads.
   - `/examination` — Examination centre with notifications, schedules, RAU results portal link, and practical viva information.
   - `/academics` — Academic division overview with BAMS 100 Seats, MD Kayachikitsa, and 14 classical departments.
   - `/hospital` — 100-bed attached hospital, 8 clinical OPDs, Panchakarma sanctum, casualty 24x7 helpline.
   - Subpages: `/about/organization-structure`, `/about/annual-reports`, `/about/policies`, `/academics/calendar`, `/academics/methodology`, `/academics/practical-training`, `/research/activities`, `/research/committees`, `/research/collaborations`.
3. **Mandatory Disclosure Center (`/mandatory-disclosures`)**:
   - Comprehensive NCISM Minimum Standard Requirements (MSR) declaration covering all 16 statutory sections.
4. **Institutional Footer Synchronization (`Footer.tsx`)**:
   - Harmonized all footer link columns to reflect the new 9 master pillars.
5. **Testing & Verification**:
   - TypeScript verification (`npx tsc --noEmit`): **Passed with 0 errors**.
   - HTTP Status check across 38 primary routes: **All 38 returned HTTP 200 OK**.
   - Broken links: **0**.
