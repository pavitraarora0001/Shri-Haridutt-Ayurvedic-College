## 2. INTEGRITY POLICY: UNAVAILABLE / PENDING CONTENT

2. **Pending Documents State:** Any upcoming academic session orders undergoing annual renewal are explicitly flagged as `"Document Under Annual Verification with Regulatory Directorate"` rather than omitted or falsified.
3. **Certified True Copies:** Available on request through the Principal's Directorate.
