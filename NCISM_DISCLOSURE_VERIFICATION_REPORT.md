## 1. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

Under Section 28 & 29 of the NCISM Act 2020 and Chapter 4 of the NCISM Minimum Standard Requirements (MSR) Regulations, every recognized Ayurvedic Medical College must maintain an active, publicly accessible **Mandatory Disclosure Portal** on its official website.

The portal implemented at [`/mandatory-disclosures`](file:///c:/Pavitra-Arora/Royal-Business-Machine/Website/Shri%20Haridutt%20Ayurvedic%20College/src/app/mandatory-disclosures/page.tsx) was audited against the 16 statutory disclosure schedules.
