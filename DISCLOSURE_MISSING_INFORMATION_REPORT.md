## 1. Regulatory Context

Under Section 28 of the NCISM Act 2020, institutions must publish verified records and flag any information requiring periodic regulatory update or pending council gazette. This report audits data availability, confidentiality boundaries, and items scheduled for future academic update.

## 3. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Compliance Note

In compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)**:
1. No personal telephone numbers of students, parents, or faculty are publicly exposed.
2. Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, and bank account details are strictly excluded from all public tables and downloadable PDF gazettes.
3. Student examination results are displayed in aggregate pass percentages and merit group recognitions without exposing private academic transcripts.
4. Only officially sanctioned institutional contact channels (`principal@hariduttayurveda.edu.in`, `+91 98290 44120`) are published for public inquiries.
